Use your Atlantic, Thermor and Sauter appliances in Homey. Log in with the account you use in the Cozytouch app and Homey finds your water heaters, boilers, heat pumps, air conditioners and towel racks.

Check how much hot water is left before you shower. Turn on boost when guests stay over, or switch between manual, Eco+ and program mode. With Flows you can get a notification when hot water drops below a level you pick, or put the boiler in away mode when everyone leaves the house. Homey checks your devices every minute.

Supported devices

Water heaters: Atlantic Explorer V5 200L, 240L, 270L and 270L Coil, Atlantic Aqueo ACI Hyb VS 300L and VM 150L, Atlantic Calypso Split and Calypso 200L, Thermor Aeromax Split 3, Thermor Aeromax 6, Thermor Malicio 3 120L, Thermor Lineo Connecte MP 100L and Sauter Phazy.

Gas boilers: Atlantic Naema 2 Micro 25, Naema 2 Duo 25, Naema 3 Micro 25 and Naia 2 Micro 25.

Heat pumps: Atlantic Alfea Extensa Duo AI UE and Alfea Extensa Duo A.I. 3 R32.

Thermostats: Atlantic Navilink Connect and Loria Duo 6006.

Air conditioners: Atlantic and Thermor Takao M3 and Naviclim.

Towel racks: Atlantic Kelud 1750W, Doris Etroit 1500W, Asama Connecte II Ventilo 1500W and 1750W, and Thermor Riva 5.

Is your model not listed? Other Cozytouch devices often work too. If yours doesn't, report the model through the support link on this page and we'll look into adding it.
